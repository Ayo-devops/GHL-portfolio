# SECTION 07: MY APPROACH

**HOW I WORK**

## I don't want to just build something that looks good in a demo.

I've seen how easy it is to create an impressive-looking workflow that falls apart the moment real customers start using it.

That's why I prefer a more thoughtful process.

### First, I listen.

Before touching your account, I want to understand your business, what's currently happening, what isn't working, and what you'd like to improve.

### Then, we map things out.

We look at the customer journey, the tools involved, and the steps that need to happen. This helps us build around real needs instead of assumptions.

### Next, I build and connect the pieces.

Whether it's a CRM setup, funnel, AI agent, or complex workflow, I focus on making the system practical, organized, and aligned with what we've agreed to achieve.

### After that, we test.

Because a workflow that looks complete isn't necessarily a workflow that works. I check the important paths, triggers, conditions, and handoffs before considering the job ready.

### Finally, I help you take control.

I explain what's been built, how it works, and what your team needs to know.

My goal isn't to make you dependent on me for every little change.

**It's to leave you with a system you understand and can confidently use.**

# SECTION 11: FAQ

**A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING**

### Do I need an existing GoHighLevel account before working with you?

Not necessarily. If you're starting fresh, I can help you plan and configure your setup. If you already have an account, we can review what's there and determine what needs to be improved.

### Can you fix an existing GHL setup without rebuilding everything?

Yes, when the existing structure allows it. I'll review the issue, identify what's causing the problem, and recommend the most practical approach. Sometimes that's a targeted fix. Other times, restructuring is the better long-term choice.

### Do you work with agencies or only individual businesses?

Both. I've worked on different kinds of implementations and can support agencies needing technical GHL assistance as well as businesses building their own CRM and automation systems.

### Can you integrate GoHighLevel with other platforms?

Yes. Depending on the platform and available integration methods, I can work with native integrations, webhooks, APIs, and external automation tools. I'll confirm feasibility before committing to a particular connection.

### How long will my project take?

That depends on what we're building, the complexity involved, and what's already available. A targeted workflow fix is very different from a complete CRM and automation build. Once I understand your requirements, I can give you a realistic scope and timeline.

### Will you explain how to use the system after the project?

Absolutely. I want you to understand what has been built and how the important parts work. We'll agree on the appropriate handoff or training as part of your project scope.

### What if I'm not sure what I need yet?

That's completely fine. You don't need to know the technical solution before reaching out. Tell me what's happening in your business and what you're trying to improve. We can figure out what makes sense from there.
